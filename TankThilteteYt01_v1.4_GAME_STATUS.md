## What kind of game this is

It's a **3D auto-aim tank survival arena shooter**, made for phone screens in **portrait orientation** with touch controls.

Think of it as a "one-thumb-friendly" survival shooter. You drive a tank around a big open battlefield. Your cannon **aims and shoots by itself** — you never aim manually. Your only real jobs are:

- **Driving** (move, dodge shells, use cover),
- **Positioning** (hide behind trees/rocks, funnel enemies into your auto-aim),
- **Choosing upgrades** (pick cards when you level up).

One "session" works like this:

> Home screen → pick difficulty → survive as long as you can → level up by picking upgrade cards → fight a boss every 5 levels → die or quit → spend coins in the shop → start a stronger run.

Every run makes your tank permanently a little stronger, so the loop is **die → get stronger → die later**.

The entire game is **one HTML file (~1 MB)** that runs offline. The 3D engine (*Three.js*) is embedded inside the file. All sound is generated by code — there are no audio files. Progress is saved in the phone's browser storage.

## The screens

- **Home screen** — a hangar backdrop with your tank on a spinning display platform (it wears your equipped skin and even shows the upgrade parts). Includes a title, three stat cells, and buttons: *Play*, *Shop*, *Settings*, *Load Save*. A small line shows which skin is equipped.
- **In-game screen** — the battlefield with a top HUD:
  - **HP bar** (turns red below 30%, amber below 60%, pulses when critical), shown as a **number** like `47/100` that rises when you upgrade (e.g. `80/120`); the number never rounds up (so it won't falsely show "100/100" while you're still regenerating); **v1.2** adds a small **armor bar** (blue) right underneath it with its own `x/y` value,
  - **XP bar**, shown as a **number** like `36/120`, plus **level chip**, **score**, **coins**,
  - a **Damage / Speed meter** (mini bars showing your *real* current damage % and speed % — including the effect of Overcharge, Blast, Haste, Adrenaline, and the roots biome's slow; the bar fills to match the number, so 100% is a full bar, and it shows a green **BOOST** or amber **SLOWED** label while a speed change is active),
  - a small **radar minimap** (tinted with the current biome's fog color, with glowing dots for supply drops and a pulsing ring around bosses),
  - a **combo counter** with a 3-second decay bar,
  - small **buff pills** (shield, overcharge, haste, bounty).
  - Bosses show a slim HP bar along the top.
- **Pause menu** — resume, quick-save, settings, quit. It shows your build cleanly: core stats, an **UPGRADES** list with each skill once and a count (e.g. `Missile ×2`), and an **EVOLUTIONS** list below it. Tapping an evolution opens a detail page showing what it needs, what you've already picked, and whether it's active. The armor stat reads like `🛡️ 0 armor` so it's clear it's a soak shield, not a level number.
- **Death screen** — run stats (level, kills, coins, best score, **bosses killed**, **highest combo**), your build summary, a shop shortcut, and a **revive button** (pay coins to keep the run going).
- **Shop / Armory** — permanent upgrades, tank skins, and one-run consumables.
- **Settings** — sound, music, graphics quality, camera mode, screen-shake (three levels), reduced-flash mode, left-handed controls, control assist, haptics.

## The core loop, step by step

1. **Drive** — a joystick occupies the left half of the screen. Push up and the tank moves "up" on the screen. The camera glides behind you and leans into your direction of travel when you're moving fast.
2. **Fire** — touch and hold the right half. The cannon auto-picks a target and shoots. You never aim manually.
3. **Kill** — kills give points, coins, and XP. Quick kills in a row build a **combo** (decays after 3 seconds of no kills); a high combo multiplies your coin payout up to **2.2×**.
4. **Level up** — each level you get **3 upgrade cards and pick 1** (you can reroll the hand if you bought the perk). This is where builds snowball. **v1.1/v1.2:** for your **first 3 upgrades** the hand always includes Health Regen (Nano Repair) and Heal-on-Kill (Field Medic) plus one other random card, so early runs never starve for survivability.
5. **Boss every 5 levels** — one boss at a time, with a **12-second breather** afterward. Beating one heals you **25%** and pays out a special **boss vault** of amplified ("ELITE") cards.
6. **The world changes every 3 levels** — a new biome fades in.
7. **Die or quit → bank coins → shop → repeat.**

## Camera and visuals

The view is a **high-angle "3/4" chase camera**. It sits behind and slightly above your tank (about 38° up, a fixed direction — it never rotates with the tank). So you read the field like a map while the tanks still look fully 3D.

It subtly **zooms in** when enemies are close, **zooms out** when the field is empty, zooms out a bit more during **boss fights**, and **shudders** on big impacts.

Tanks are built from simple 3D shapes (boxes, cylinders, cones) with a metallic paint job — no texture images, which keeps it fast on phones. The tank has two pivots: the **hull** turns with your driving, and the **turret** (with the barrel) turns independently toward whatever the auto-aim picked.

## How the auto-aim works (the "AI that aims for you")

Every frame the game picks your target with a simple, readable rule set:

- Over a **10-second cycle**: for 5 seconds it alternates between a "smart target" (boss first → most dangerous → lowest health → closest) and "just the closest thing," re-choosing its mode about every **0.85 seconds**. For the other 5 seconds it simply targets the closest enemy. This makes the aiming feel alive rather than robotic.
- If only **1 or 2 enemies** are nearby (within ~16 units), local sense takes over: boss > most dangerous > closest.
- A **25% "stickiness"** discount keeps the current target a moment longer, so the gun doesn't twitch between two equally close enemies.
- Dead or gone targets are dropped instantly.
- The turret rotates smoothly toward the target (faster if you turned on **Control Assist**).

## Your tank and its stats

Your power comes from three layers:

1. **Base stats** — grown permanently in the shop.
2. **Run cards** — picked at level-ups, last one run.
3. **Evolutions** — special run bonuses unlocked by combos of cards.

**What each stat actually does:**

- **Max HP / Regen** — your health pool and health-per-second recovery.
- **Armor** — a soak shield. It absorbs **all** incoming damage until it is reduced to zero, and **then** the excess hits your health. It refills over time at a rate tied to your regen stat, and is restored to full by repair kits, shield batteries, and airdrops. A small blue bar under your HP shows how much armor is left (and flashes `ARMOR DOWN` when it breaks).
- **Damage** — each shell hits harder. **Fire rate** — shells leave the barrel faster.
- **Speed** — top driving speed. **v1.4:** speed is capped at 260% of base so stacked speed cards can't make the tank uncontrollable (the meter reflects the same cap). **Crit** — chance a shell deals double damage (shows a "CRIT" popup).
- **Multishot** — extra parallel projectiles per shot. **Pierce** — a shell keeps going through enemies after the first hit.
- **Missiles** — a homing missile launcher on a timer; more stacks fire **more missiles per launch** (1, then 2, then 3).
- **Splash** — shells create an explosion with a ~6.5-unit blast radius instead of single-target damage.
- **Shield** — a bubble that absorbs one full hit, then recharges on a timer (shows "SHIELD READY" + a vibration when back).
- **Scavenger** — more coins from kills. **Bounty Hunter** — more XP from kills (faster levels).
- **Field Medic** — heal a few HP on every kill. **Adrenaline** — a speed burst after every kill (each stack: +25% speed for 1.5s, and **v1.4** also +5% damage while the burst is live, so even one stack is worth taking).

**Evolutions** — 6 special run bonuses that appear on your card choices only after you've collected the right *counts* of cards (stacks matter, not just ownership):

- **Cluster Warheads** (1 Missile + 1 Splash) — missiles burst into 3 bomblets.
- **Bastion Core** (2 Armor + 1 Shield) — after your shield pops, it still soaks 25% of the next hit.
- **Prism Cannon** (1 Pierce + 1 Crit) — crits punch through one extra enemy.
- **Nanite Harvest** (1 Regen + 2 Field Medic) — kills heal even more.
- **Afterburner** (2 Speed + 1 Adrenaline) — the post-kill speed burst lasts 3 seconds and is stronger.
- **Siege Loader** (2 Damage + 2 Fire Rate) — every 4th volley is a heavy slug doing 70% more damage.

## Enemies

There are **33 enemy types**, built from the same tank parts, each with its own speed, armor, damage, and role. They spawn around the screen edges and hunt you.

Broadly:

- **Fast swarmers** — small, quick, weak; they come in packs.
- **Standard fighters** — steady chasers with average stats.
- **Ranged types** — they keep their distance and shoot at you. **Snipers** (very long barrel, shots telegraphed with a warning line so you can dodge) and **artillery**.
- **Heavy hitters** — slow, armored, high damage; one also telegraphs its shots.
- **Support** — a healer type that repairs nearby allies.
- **Shielded** — a reinforced front plate that shrugs off frontal hits.
- **Kamikaze** — rushes you and explodes on contact.
- **Trappers** — a minelayer that leaves mines behind, which blow up if you drive over them.
- **Elites** — from level 15, about 14% of spawns are elites: glowing cyan, stronger, worth more.
- All enemy fire scales slightly with level. A "hush" system briefly silences enemy fire during biome changes and after revives so you always get a fair breather.

Destructible **trees and rocks** are scattered around; they **block bullets**, so hiding behind them is real strategy (the game teaches you this with a tip during your first boss fight).

## The six bosses

A boss arrives every 5 levels (only one at a time, normal spawns speed up while one lives). Each has a distinct pattern and **two phases — at 60% HP and 30% HP it gets visibly angrier**:

- **Warlord** (first boss) — fires a fan of shells. *Phase 2:* wider, faster fan. *Phase 3:* double barrages plus two scout reinforcements. **v1.1:** its shells were tuned down — projectile speed and damage both halved so the first boss is fair on a fresh run.
- **Colossus** — bursts of shells and summons reinforcements. *Phase 2:* bigger waves. *Phase 3:* an extra triple-burst every 10 seconds.
- **Nova** — fires a ring of shells in all directions and "cloaks" (fades) while charging. *Phase 2:* double rings. *Phase 3:* triple rings plus a teleport dash.
- **Titan** — a bruiser that slams the ground for a shockwave, blinks across the field, and tracks you with follow-up slams.
- **Tempest** — a blink striker: vanishes, reappears close to you, and attacks; its late phase adds a second blink.
- **Fortress** — a slow turret that paints the ground with a spiral of shells in timed bursts; late phases spin faster and add counter-rotating arms, turning the pattern into a maze.

Boss attacks are telegraphed (warning lines) where it makes sense, so you're never surprised by a completely invisible projectile.

## Biomes

There are **10 biomes**, and the world changes to the next one every 3 levels. Biomes are **visual themes, not rule-sets**. Each brings its own ground color, fog color/distance, sun light, tree/rock styles, and drifting ambient particles.

The transition is a smooth "morph" — the old world shrinks away as the new one scales in. During it, enemy fire is briefly muted and no new enemies spawn for 3 seconds, so the change never catches you mid-fight. (The radar background is tinted with the current biome's fog color.) **v1.1:** water has been removed from every biome — no body of water appears anywhere.

## Pickups and drops

- **Supply crates** (gold, from level 1) fall from the sky on a beam of light, land with a bounce, and glow on the minimap. Drive into one for one of six rewards: **repair** (+45% HP **and restores armor**), **overcharge** (damage boost for 15s), **shield** (instant full shield **and restores armor**), **coin cache**, **bonus upgrade card**, or **haste** (10s speed). Crates are more common early and thin out as you level. **v1.1:** airdrops now start from level 1, and for the first 5 levels they give the repair (health) reward 70% of the time; after level 5 the reward is fully random.
- **Black-market crates** (purple, ~1 in 4) open a small shop overlay with extra purchases, bordered in gold — a bit riskier, a bit richer.
- **Aid orbs** (floating glowing spheres): **green = repair**, **blue = shield**, **cyan = haste**. More common early, rare later.
- At most **2 crates and 2 orbs** exist at once, and both show as pulsing dots on the radar so you can plan routes around them.

## Time-based events

- **Enemy surges** — a timed pressure wave announces itself; enemies spawn in a rapid rhythm for ~15 seconds, and if you survive the window you collect a coin bounty.
- **Fun events** (from level 8 onward): **Bounty** (high-value enemies), **Ambush** (a group appears at once around you), **Raid** (from level 10), **Lull** (from level 15 — a short 6-second truce to recover).

## The shop (three sections)

Coins earned in runs buy three kinds of things:

1. **Armory (permanent upgrades, 15 items)** — these grow your *base* tank forever: starting HP, damage, speed, armor, regen (repair kits, level 6 cap), **Second Wind** (auto-revive once per run; level 2 revives at 75% HP), **Extra Choice** (+1 reroll per level-up), plus seven **unlimited** upgrades whose prices rise with each purchase (small % damage, +HP, +armor, +regen, +fire rate, +crit). Buying on the death screen still applies to a revive you take right after.
2. **Skins (6)** — pure cosmetics: Amber (free), Crimson, Emerald, Glacier, Void Walker, 24k Commander. Equipping one instantly recolors your tank everywhere, even on the home-screen display model.
3. **Consumables (one-run boosts, 5 items, unlimited copies)** — **Lucky Charm** (+20% coins next run), **Head Start** (start with one free card), **Card Reroll** (one extra reroll), **Overcharge** (+30% damage for the first 60 seconds), **Aegis Kit** (start with a charged shield).

**Economy rule:** every coin income source (kills, crates, surges, achievements) passes through a single **10% tax**, so the whole economy is tuned from one place. Prices rise with each purchase of the same item, so you balance which upgrades to chase.

## Achievements and records

**14 lifetime achievements** (first kill, 500 kills, first boss, 10 bosses, 100 crits, 100 destroyed objects, 5,000 units traveled, 25,000 lifetime coins, reach level 20, reach level 30, an 8-kill combo, 15 minutes played, beat a boss on Nightmare, own 3 skins) — each pays a coin reward once.

Lifetime stats (kills, distance, playtime, best score, best run) persist across everything, and the death screen celebrates new personal bests.

## Difficulty and comeback mechanics

- **Four difficulties** — Easy / Normal / Hard / Nightmare — scale how hard enemies hit you and how often they fire (~0.7× to 1.6×). Higher difficulties are required for some achievements.
- **Revive (continue):** on the death screen you can pay coins to keep the exact run going — you respawn where you died at 50% HP with 3 seconds of invulnerability, a 3-second no-spawn grace, all live bullets cleared, and your missile timer reset. The price **quadruples** each revive (300, then 1,200, then 4,800…), so it's a real decision.
- **Saves:** progress autosaves constantly; you can also name and save manual save slots, quick-save from the pause menu, and load saves from the death screen or home.

## Comfort and accessibility

- **Haptics** — the phone vibrates on meaningful moments (level up, evolution picked, shield ready, hit blocked, boss down), with a master toggle.
- **Camera shake in 3 levels** (full / reduced / off), **reduced flash mode** (damage flashes last 45 ms instead of 80 ms), **left-handed layout** (swaps which hand drives and which fires), **control assist** (turret tracks targets faster).
- **Graphics quality** — High / Low / **Auto**. Auto watches the battlefield (enemy count, bullet count, particle count, late levels) and drops to Low only when the load stays heavy for a few seconds, then restores it when things calm down (with a small notification each time).
- **First-run tips** — the first few runs show gentle toasts: how to drive, how to fire, what cards mean, where crates come from, and the cover tip during your first boss.

## Tech under the hood (one paragraph)

Everything lives in one HTML file: an embedded copy of *Three.js* for 3D, procedurally generated sound (no audio files), a 2D canvas minimap, and browser local storage for saves. It's built mobile-first for portrait phones with safe-area padding, targeting **60 FPS** on mid-range phones (capped pixel ratio, shared scratch math objects, chunked tasks across frames). It ships with a **PWA shell**: a web manifest (`manifest.webmanifest`), the two icons, an offline service worker (`sw.js`), and an `index.html` entry point that opens the current versioned game — so over HTTPS it installs like an app and plays without internet.
